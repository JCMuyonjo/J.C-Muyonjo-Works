Research & Projects In Development

AR Ugandan Sounds

Augmented Reality & Digital Cataloging

Preserving East African cultural heritage through interactive 3D augmented reality musical instruments—building complementary tools for museums and heritage preservation.

Read Research Thesis →

Re-Imagining Twilight

Folger Artistic Fellowship (Washington D.C.)

Audio-visual project re-examining Ruth B. Fisher's 1911 work "Twilight Tales of the Black Baganda". Combining paintings, field recordings, and digital audio-reactive soundscapes to challenge biased historical narratives about Ugandan indigenous musical heritage.
